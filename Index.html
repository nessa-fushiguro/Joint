Frequently Asked Questions

How does Joint work?

×

Joint connects students with similar routes. You can find drivers or passengers, request a ride, and travel together safely.

What is the business model?

+

We charge a small commission per ride and partner with universities through licenses.

Is Joint safe?

+

All users are verified with university credentials to ensure a secure community.

Who can use Joint?

+

Joint is exclusively for university students and staff.

How do payments work?

+

Payments are handled securely through the app's integrated wallet.

Can I be both driver and passenger?

+

Yes, you can switch roles easily depending on your needs for the day.
